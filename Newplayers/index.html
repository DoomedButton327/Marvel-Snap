1 Fixtures & Scheduling

Fixtures are sent between 00:00–10:00 SAST (GMT+2), Monday–Saturday, excluding cup days and public holidays. Deadline for results is 23:59 same day. NO LATE SUBMISSIONS. You cannot send a first message after 19:00. Fixtures show the first 4 letters of each opponent's WhatsApp username.

2 Result Submission

Submit a screenshot of your match result (final Cube count / win screen) within a 15‑minute grace period after the match ends. If your opponent didn't respond, submit screenshot evidence of that attempt within a 6‑hour grace period (only opens after 19:00). If nothing is submitted, the game is recorded as a draw. Tag both admins in the Mettlestate group. All league messages must go on the Mettlestate group — no direct messaging.

3 No‑Shows & Forfeits

If an opponent doesn't arrive at the agreed time, automatic forfeit after a 10‑minute grace period. If your opponent sets a time and is 30 minutes late, you are awarded the win. Failure to follow this process results in a forfeit or draw at admin discretion.

4 Postponements

Each player may postpone 20 matches per season. Exceptions for valid reasons (device issues, family emergency, etc.). If a player is unreachable mid‑session and both submit screenshot evidence, the result is a draw or postponement at admin discretion.

5 Disconnections

If disconnected mid‑match, immediately report to the league admin and your opponent to arrange a rematch. If network or app issues are the cause, provide screenshots of the match state at disconnection, or a video if possible. No evidence = straight forfeit. No excuses.

6 Disputes & Admin

All disputes are handled by the organisers, helpers, and admin. Raise it in the group. Admin decisions are final. Rules may be updated between seasons at organiser discretion. Visit mettlestate.com for community and general rules.
